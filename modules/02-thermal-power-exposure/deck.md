# Thermal power exposure screening
## 1. Decision question
Can a transparent asset-level screen connect heat hazard to an exposure inventory?

## 2. Public inputs
WRI Global Power Plant Database asset attributes; World Bank CCKP CMIP6 indicator methodology. Accessed 13 September 2026.

## 3. Method
Screening score = capacity × annual hot-day signal × cooling vulnerability weight. Once-through cooling = 1.40; recirculating cooling = 0.90. This is a prioritisation index, not expected loss.

## 4. Results
Andalusia West ranks highest (27,720); Aegean North follows (20,664). The 3,570 MW sample has a capacity-weighted score of 15.04.

## 5. Interpretation
The screen shows why exposure integration matters: equal hazard does not imply equal vulnerability when cooling technology differs.

## 6. Limitations and next step
Small illustrative extract, no geocoding or downscaling, no financial loss function. Next step: replace the extract with the complete public inventory and join gridded hazard data.
